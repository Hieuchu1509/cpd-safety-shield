## Overall Impression

Two projects in this folder; one was designed. The authorship is real — restrained Vietnamese script honoring locked numbers, a state sequence that proves causality, a bespoke audio engine with a heartbeat ramping 72 to 105 to 130 bpm. Around it sits a stock dark analytics dashboard, restyled three times, ~40% of which is a tool for making the video rather than the video's argument.

Biggest opportunity: THE PIECE HAS NO ENDING. app.js:722 calls togglePlay() after scene 8, restoring the play overlay with a START prompt. Exactly one anchor element in the entire body — the skip link. No end card, no CTA, no share. PRODUCT.md's success condition (a parent who wants CPD in their car) has no mechanism by which it can be satisfied.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the piece must land in silence on a 278px image, why is it a video player at all? Autoplay-with-timed-advance is wrong for BOTH confirmed contexts. What if the phone experience were scroll-driven and only the booth got timed autoplay?
2. The escalation ladder is the argument — why has it no visual form in 3,804 lines of CSS? What if the ladder WERE the primary composition, with the eight stills hung off its rungs as evidence?
3. Whose POV is the camera in? The HUD renders the child as TARGET #01. What if it were reframed as the car trying to reach someone, rather than a sensor logging a target?
4. What is the one sentence a parent should say to their spouse that evening? The build hedges across three takeaways and lands none.
5. The piece maximizes fear and offers nothing to do with it — isn't that the real ethical problem, more than any framing choice? What would it mean to design the ending FIRST and build the 108 seconds backwards from it?
